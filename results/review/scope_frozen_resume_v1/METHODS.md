## DEV threshold and evaluation
Unified threshold=8.367393; strict posterior mean>threshold defines detection. For each of seven DEV zero-K32 background strata, source groups receive equal total weight; compute a95th weighted quantile then use the maximum across strata. Thus easy negatives cannot dilute difficult backgrounds. Actual achieved DEV FPR per stratum is saved. The same threshold applies to both references on TEST. q05>0 is never used as positivity.

Quantities: mean/median count MAE,RMSE,bias,slope/intercept,auxiliary Spearman; Epi_other and total epithelial errors; draw-wise fractions; negative distributions/FPR; count×depth×epithelial composition detection; paired reference differences; state-intensity paired effects. Overall and subgroup metrics give equal total weight to each source sample. Per-sample and conservative-group results are retained. Conditional rates average equally over samples with the relevant denominator: negative spots for FPR, positive spots for recall, and detected spots for precision; undefined per-source precision is omitted rather than set to zero. Spearman correlation is an auxiliary pooled-spot descriptive statistic. No narrow pseudo-replicate confidence interval is reported. State-intensity exploratory signed-rank uses source-group means only when exact-test prerequisites hold and at least3 groups exist; these are unverified source groups, not confirmed independent patients. Simulated positive prevalence is reported alongside precision/recall and is not real spatial prevalence.

## Reproducibility and limits
Master seed123; deterministic purpose-specific SHA256-derived seeds are recorded, with identical paired-reference seeds. Inputs, cells, labels, splits, genes, settings, signatures and pseudospots have hashes. Sparse counts are cached; no full gene×cell dense matrix is materialized. Limited minibatches/reconstruction subsets may be dense as required by NB likelihood. Models share one GPU under the later explicit user authorization for resource-aware concurrency; the exact scheduling override is recorded in RUNTIME_CONCURRENCY_OVERRIDE.md. Output stays in scope_frozen_resume_v1; historical results remain read-only.

K32 comes from the full historical discovery set. This is transfer/mixture recovery of a frozen candidate, not independently rediscovered labels. Pseudospots do not reproduce all scRNA-to-Visium platform effects. K32 abundance is not all MP10-high abundance. Conclusions first apply to this frozen scope and simulation conditions. No classifier, NMF/UCell/PCA/Harmony/clustering/UMAP rerun, CNV/prognosis/CellChat/niche or real spatial fit was performed. Training success and identifiability judgement are distinct.
