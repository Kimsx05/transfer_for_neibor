| [Seurat HD segmented cells](https://satijalab.org/seurat/articles/visiumhd_analysis_cell_segmentations) | Same-region cell/bin comparison and RCTD doublet analysis | Five pre-existing ROIs are frozen before bin predictions. No new segmentation, cell mapping or expression inference. |

Limited paired subset uses installed sctransform 0.4.3 v2, nb_offset fallback, ncells <= 5,000, no residual input. This is sensitivity analysis, not exact reproduction. |
| [D1/Thor paper](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-025-62593-1), [author notebook](https://github.com/GuangyuWangLab2021/Thor/blob/main/visiumhd/Comprehensive_tutorial_VisiumHD.ipynb) | Notebook loads 8 μm input and normalizes total to 10,000, then log1p | nCount > 5 and our clustering/sketch settings are common project controls, not confirmed D1-paper QC/cluster settings. | [SingleR](https://bioconductor.org/books/release/SingleRBook/annotation-diagnostics.html) | Fine-tuned labels, delta.next, default pruning after merged queries | Frozen BLCA/HPCA training objects and gene handling from the cell diagnostic run. Scores are correlations, not calibrated confidence. |
| [Original spacexr](https://github.com/dmcable/spacexr) | Reference / SpatialRNA / create.RCTD / run.RCTD API, doublet mode | Installed original spacexr 2.2.1 in this result directory. Default UMI_min=100, UMI_min_sigma=300, counts_MIN=10; these algorithm gates are reported separately from bin QC. Max 4 workers; internal T_NK converted back to T/NK in output. |

Raw counts retain all 18,085 genes. BLCA retains Ensembl IDs; HPCA temporarily merges duplicate symbols by raw-count sum then renormalizes. The three duplicated symbols are TBCE, HSPA14 and TMSB15B. Original input IDs and mappings remain in each section's gene_mapping.tsv. Models are reused from ../hd_annotation_diagnosis_singler_v1/reference (BLCA 44,896 cells; nine major classes; HPCA ten native main classes merged to six comparison classes). HPCA main-class training cannot independently resolve Mast, Plasma or Mural.

No object-to-bin nearest-centroid correspondence is used. The native 2 μm grid maps to its 8 μm parent through floor(array_row/4), floor(array_col/4), origin (0,0). Counts are conserved per gene on the supplied 2 μm input. Native 8 μm versus supplied 2 μm differences are kept separate as mask/input differences. Coordinates use the original verified transformations, including D1 crop origin (23764,35851); physical y increases downward, Seurat FOV and plots use -y.

ROI panel co-detection means measured signal from the specified gene groups, not cell identity or proven contamination. Four-unit signal panels show raw 2 μm, original 4 μm assignment, current 8 μm assignment, and fixed 8 μm grid. Cell4 and cell8 use the same original v2 analysis-object IDs. Cyan outlines are original nuclei and gray outlines are current v2 8 μm assignment regions in every panel; gray outlines do not represent 4 μm regions. No smoothing or spatial label voting is used. ROI statistics use centers within fixed bounds; whole-object expression may include assigned bins beyond a bound, so ROI totals across different units are descriptive rather than conservation identities.

Plots use the installed jins-routine-plotting shared theme/export and UMAP template. Spatial/ROI and matrix-comparison figures have no exact supplied template and use the same shared style. Dense layers and H&E are raster where appropriate; text and legends stay vector in PDF. White background, Arial, 300 dpi PNG; legend-bearing figures include full, legend-only and panel-only PDFs. Counts and raw-expression matrices are not duplicated for review packaging.
